# Stage 8 Sequence/Context Sample Plan v0.1

Status: FROZEN DESIGN TARGET. This document does not authorize model training.

## Initial corpus size

The first Stage 8 sequence/context ambiguity research program targets 1,200 human-verified cases:

- 800 TRAIN
- 200 VALIDATION
- 200 untouched SC-HOLDOUT

The untouched SC-HOLDOUT must be frozen before model selection and must not be used for threshold tuning, feature selection, hyperparameter selection, or target derivation.

## License policy

OpenScore String Quartets and OpenScore Lieder are accepted for v0.1 only because their official repositories state that the scores are released under Creative Commons Zero (CC0). Exact repository snapshot identifiers and content SHA-256 values must still be recorded before extraction.

The project-owned synthetic source must be generated by project-authored code/templates or explicitly owned material. Generated intent is not accepted as the final target label: every retained ambiguity case still requires human musical verification under the SC corpus contract.

DCML/ABC and related DCML harmonic corpora are CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 and therefore remain REFERENCE_ONLY under the commercial-safe Stage 8 governance policy. MAESTRO and ASAP are likewise CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 and receive no v0.1 TRAIN, VALIDATION, or SC-HOLDOUT allocation.

Mutopia is intentionally excluded as a bulk v0.1 source because licensing varies by item (for example Public Domain, CC BY, and CC BY-SA entries). It may be reconsidered only through an item-level rights manifest; no bulk inference from composer public-domain status is permitted.

## Sampling and leakage rules

1. Only frames whose deterministic Stage 7/8 core output remains `AMBIGUOUS` are eligible.
2. The immutable deterministic candidate set is the only set a human may rank.
3. Human review may choose one existing candidate or `no_preference`; no forced labels.
4. Source groups must be disjoint across TRAIN, VALIDATION, and SC-HOLDOUT.
5. Synthetic variants derived from the same progression/template family must remain in one split.
6. For real scores, an entire work/source group must remain in one split; windows from the same work may not cross splits.
7. SC-HOLDOUT should be composer-disjoint from TRAIN/VALIDATION where practical, and at minimum work/source-group disjoint.
8. Teacher-Gold and its HOLDOUT remain completely disjoint and are never copied into the SC corpus.
9. Raw copyrighted score/audio payloads are not committed to this public repository; only approved metadata/fingerprints may be committed.

## Why 1,200 cases

The initial model target is a small, explainable candidate ranker rather than a deep generative model. Eight hundred training cases provide a controlled first baseline for a bounded feature set, while 200 validation and 200 untouched holdout cases preserve separate model-selection and final-generalization measurements. If uncertainty remains too wide or important ambiguity families are underrepresented, expansion must be preregistered as a new corpus version rather than silently adding cases after seeing SC-HOLDOUT results.

## Promotion boundary

This plan only fixes corpus design. It does not authorize feature extraction, training, model selection, shadow deployment, or production authority. Training remains blocked until rights manifests, split manifests, human verification, preregistration hashes, and the separate untouched SC-HOLDOUT contract all pass.
